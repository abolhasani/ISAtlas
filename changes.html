Release history

What changed in each Atlas version.

The version is visible throughout the site and will be updated with every released change.

v0.3.3July 26, 2026

Citation-flow correction

  • Focused graphs now show citation flow consistently: cited resources flow into the focal paper, while papers citing the focal paper receive arrows from it.
v0.3.2July 26, 2026

Citation direction and paginated search

  • Edges from cited resources now point into the focal paper.
  • Search results support 20, 50, or 100 records per page, with page tracking and Back/Next navigation.
v0.3.1July 26, 2026

Scrollable paper details and cumulative trends

  • Made long focal-paper detail panels independently scrollable on small screens.
  • Added a cumulative papers and citations trend chart to Insights.
  • Enabled empty-space dragging to pan a zoomed graph.
v0.3.0July 26, 2026

Completed citation data and research tools

  • Rebuilt browser data for the complete direct citing and cited-paper collection, with external metadata loaded in on-demand bundles.
  • Added per-paper citation downloads, refocusing between focal-journal papers, and optional left-to-right citation flow layouts.
  • Added FAQ and Insights pages, active search-filter chips, CSV exports, mobile-safe graph controls, and a main-site footer link.
v0.2.5July 25, 2026

Journal-aware year ranges

  • The lower bound of the publication-year fields and sliders moves to the earliest available paper for the selected journal or journal combination.
v0.1.0July 25, 2026

Atlas foundation

  • Interactive graph, search and extraction workspace, themes, version history, and local browser data bundles.
